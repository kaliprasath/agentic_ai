SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is entered into between ABC Technologies
("Company") and John Smith ("Service Provider").

1. SERVICES

The Service Provider agrees to provide software development services
to the Company.

2. PAYMENT

The Company shall pay the Service Provider INR 50,000 per month.
Payment shall be made within 15 days after submission of the invoice.

3. TERM

This agreement begins on 1 January 2026 and continues for a period
of twelve months.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Service Provider shall keep confidential all business,
technical and financial information received from the Company.

5. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 30 days
written notice.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All software developed specifically for the Company under this
agreement shall belong to the Company after full payment.

7. DISPUTES

Any dispute arising from this agreement shall first be resolved
through discussion between the parties.
